The night baker has gone home. Three things kept count through the night. Press a figure to turn it face up; you have five looks. Press the STANDING ORDER to lay it under the night, and again to lift it. Put your ink to one strip, once. Then enter what was done, and at what hour. The bake goes in at 4.

OVEN

Heat on the oven, read at the end of each hour. It starts the night at 1. A stoke lifts it 2. An hour unfed lets it fall 1, never under 0. A damp log burns and lifts nothing. The flue, turned to the cupboard, costs the oven 2 that hour.

CUPBOARD

Dough against the stick, read at the end of each hour. It reads 1 the hour the batch is mixed. Each hour after, it climbs 1, or 2 if the oven ends that hour at 4 or more. A knock-back puts it at 1. The flue, turned this way, lends it 1 that hour.

STORE

What is left, counted at the end of each hour. 8 logs and 9 flour at dusk. Every stoke takes a log, damp or dry. The batch takes 3 flour when it is mixed.
